# Six-week pilot playbook

## Entry criteria

Private-sector buyer; executive sponsor; day-to-day owner; one bounded exposure; available baseline; no prohibited data; signed NDA/SOW; initial payment received.

## Timeline

- Week 0: security/data review, scope, success measures, access list.
- Week 1: exposure model and baseline; source and decision map.
- Week 2: first watchlist, operating picture, and briefing.
- Weeks 3–4: scheduled operation; relevance review; false-positive log.
- Week 5: workflow hardening, runbook, customer handoff.
- Week 6: measurement report, renewal/stop decision, permissioned impact record.

## Required measures

Lead time, relevant-alert precision, coverage, analyst hours, time-to-brief, reproducibility rate, active users, and number of decisions supported. Baseline and calculation rule are signed before results are observed.

## Stop conditions

No decision owner; inaccessible lawful data; scope expansion without change order; security requirement the product cannot meet; inability to measure; non-payment; request for unsupported certainty.

## Deliverables

Configured workspace, source registry, watchlist, scheduled briefing, incident/escalation runbook, weekly metrics, final measurement report, and annual proposal or documented stop.
