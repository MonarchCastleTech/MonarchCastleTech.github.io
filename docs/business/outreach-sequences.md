# Private-sector outreach sequences

Do not send before incorporation, confidential reply channel, CRM, and approved signature are ready.

## Initial note

Subject: Earlier warning for [exposure]

We publish open geopolitical and economic risk instruments and are building The Keep, a unified workspace for private-sector exposure. I noticed [company] operates across [specific routes/regions]. We are selecting a small number of six-week paid pilots focused on one measurable workflow—not a broad transformation project. Would a 20-minute discussion with the owner of [risk/network/exposure] be useful?

## Follow-up

One concrete pilot example: define exposed routes or countries, establish the current monitoring baseline, run source-visible alerts and briefings for six weeks, then compare lead time and analyst effort. Public products remain free; the paid work is the configured team workflow and service. If this is outside your remit, who owns the decision?

## Discovery rules

Ask about the last costly surprise, current workflow, decision owner, escalation threshold, latency, analyst time, data restrictions, budget, and renewal condition. Do not demo until the problem and owner are clear.
