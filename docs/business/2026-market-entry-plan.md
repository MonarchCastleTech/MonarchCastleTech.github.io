# Monarch Castle — Private-sector market entry plan

Status: execution baseline, 28 August 2026. No government sales in scope.

## Position

Monarch Castle sells **The Keep**, a unified early-warning workspace for private companies exposed to geopolitical, economic, energy, and supply-chain disruption. Existing public products remain free. Customers pay for coordination, private integrations, team workflows, delivery guarantees, and support.

## Initial customer profile

| Priority | Buyer | Recurring problem | First proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy/commodity risk or strategy lead | Regional and route changes reach internal decisions late | Watchlist lead time and analyst-hours saved |
| 2 | Logistics network or resilience lead | Border, port, corridor, and country signals are fragmented | Earlier disruption escalation with source trail |
| 3 | Insurance exposure manager | Emerging accumulations are detected manually | Coverage and triage-time improvement |
| 4 | Bank, fund, or advisory country-risk lead | Macro and event monitoring is duplicated | Faster sourced briefings and reproducible review |

## Commercial offer

- Public products: free, unchanged.
- Six-week paid pilot: from USD 15,000.
- Annual platform access: from USD 36,000.
- Enterprise/private deployment: from USD 75,000 plus data and infrastructure.
- Custom research is accepted only when it creates reusable platform capability.

## 90-day sequence

1. Weeks 1–2: incorporate, confirm name/mark, accounting, contracts, privacy notices, and confidential contact route.
2. Weeks 2–4: qualify 30 target accounts; conduct ten discovery calls; select three bounded pilot problems.
3. Weeks 4–10: run up to three paid pilots; record baseline, outputs, usage, lead time, and analyst effort.
4. Weeks 10–12: request permission for anonymized impact records; convert successful pilots to annual access.

## Revenue path—not a guarantee

| Stage | Customers | Average annual contract | ARR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Validation | 3 | USD 36k | USD 108k |
| Repeatability | 10 | USD 50k | USD 500k |
| Scale | 20 | USD 60k | USD 1.2m |
| Expansion | 40 | USD 75k | USD 3m |

Million-dollar revenue requires repeatable renewals and delivery capacity. It is not produced by website traffic alone.

## Non-negotiables

- No fabricated clients, testimonials, benchmarks, or certainty.
- No public-product paywall.
- No dependence on Codex or an LLM for core collection, calculation, build, or deployment.
- No government-procurement dependency.
- No confidential customer data in GitHub issues or public repositories.
- Every pilot has an owner, scope, baseline, measure, end date, and renewal decision.

## Weekly operating metrics

Qualified accounts, discovery calls, pilot proposals, paid pilots, time-to-first-output, weekly active users, analyst-hours saved, relevant-signal precision, renewals, expansion ARR, gross margin, and data/infrastructure cost per customer.
