# Pricing and unit economics

## Published starting prices

| Offer | Price | Scope boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Public portfolio | USD 0 | Current dashboards, methods, public outputs |
| Six-week pilot | From USD 15,000 | One decision workflow, one customer team |
| Team platform | From USD 36,000/year | Workspace, watchlists, briefings, exports, support |
| Enterprise | From USD 75,000/year | Private connectors, API, deployment options, service objectives |

## Pilot payment terms

50% on signature; 50% at week-three checkpoint. Taxes and third-party data/infrastructure are separate. No pilot starts without signed scope and initial payment.

## Target economics

- Pilot gross margin target: at least 60%.
- Annual software/service gross margin target: at least 75%.
- Customer acquisition payback target after repeatability: under 12 months.
- No customer should require more than 20% of roadmap capacity after pilot conversion.
- Custom work enters core product only when reusable and contractually permitted.

## Renewal gate

Renew only when the customer can name a recurring owner, workflow, and measured operational benefit. Do not discount an unproven workflow into permanence.
