PUBLISHED VOTING INTENTION

What the polls say.

Automatic polling coverage: Germany (federal and state) and the United States (2026 races). Other countries currently have calendar coverage only. Polls describe their named race, not necessarily the next election above.

Every number has a source.

Free public APIs, visible dates, and an honest account of what is covered. Election Atlas reports dates and published polls. The SDCofA Projects desk separately labels conditional AI forecasts and provisional election-night winner calls.

01 / ELECTION DATES

Precision matters.

A confirmed day, an expected month, and an expected year are different things. We preserve those distinctions. Countdown badges appear only for confirmed, day-precise dates supported by the upstream evidence. Countries without a published date remain searchable.

02 / POLLING

Polls, in context.

Charts plot individual published polls, ordered by fieldwork end date. Lines connect observations; they are not weighted averages or forecasts. Sample size, fieldwork, population or method, and commissioner are shown where supplied. Polls from different institutes and methods are not directly equivalent.

03 / UPDATES

A timestamp you can inspect.

GitHub checks the free feeds on an hourly schedule. Publishing can be delayed by upstream outages or GitHub Actions. A failed request preserves the last successful snapshot, with its original timestamp and a visible warning. “Retrieved” describes our fetch; it does not mean the source published new data.

04 / COVERAGE

A growing atlas.

The calendar reflects the upstream national feed, including some territories, referendums, and indirect votes. It is not a guarantee that every election is listed. Polling is currently limited to two countries. No account is needed to browse. AI analysis appears only in the separate projection desk; the model does not generate polling observations.
