What the model measures

The target is the party or alignment with the largest national vote share, or the largest share of reported parliamentary seats where a separate seat-only family is labelled. It does not infer seat majorities, coalition agreements or government control. Historical party labels identify blocs, not certified future candidates.

The error range uses the observed 90th-percentile leader-share error. It is not a calibrated confidence interval or a win probability. Sparse, stale and unsupported series are labelled. A single result produces only a continuity baseline.

These are retrospective tests using reconstructed current historical records, not forecasts actually published before the elections. Party reorganisations, new candidates, source revisions and regime changes limit comparability. The preset Türkiye 2028 scenario is excluded from validation.
